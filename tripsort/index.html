How it works

How to play a triple tile match

You learn it in one level. Mastering the layered boards is the fun part.

Tap a tile

It drops into your tray, which has four slots.

Match three

Three identical tiles in the tray clear on their own.

Plan ahead

Only uncovered tiles can be picked, and a full tray ends the level.

Clear and travel

Empty the board to win and move on toward the next city.

FAQ

Questions about Trip Sort

What is a triple tile match game?

A puzzle where you tap tiles to move them into a tray, and every three identical tiles clear. You win when the board is empty and lose if the tray fills up without a match. In Trip Sort the tray has four slots and every board is themed after a real city.

Can I play Trip Sort offline, without Wi-Fi?

Yes. Every level plays with no internet, no Wi-Fi and no mobile data. Offline there are simply no ads and the Shop is not available.

Learn more →
Is Trip Sort a good game to play on a plane?

Yes. It works in airplane mode, has no timers and each level takes a few minutes, so it fits anything from a short hop to a long-haul flight.

Learn more →
How do I beat a hard level?

Uncover tiles before you fill the tray, avoid leaving single tiles waiting in it, and keep boosters for when the tray is almost full. Undo, Shuffle, the Magic Wand and the extra fifth slot can each save a level. Hard Levels pay double, and every one of them can be solved.

Learn more →
Does Trip Sort train your brain?

It keeps your mind busy in a good way: you scan the board for matches, plan a few moves ahead and manage a small tray. A few levels a day make a calm brain break.

Learn more →
Is Trip Sort easy enough for seniors?

Yes. You only tap, there are no timers, there is no account to create and the first boards are gentle, so anyone can pick it up at their own pace.

Learn more →
Is Trip Sort like mahjong?

It shares the look and the calm of mahjong solitaire, with stacked tiles you uncover layer by layer. The difference is that you collect three identical tiles in a tray instead of removing pairs.

Learn more →
Is there a timer?

No. Trip Sort has no timers, so you can take as long as you like on every move. Music, sounds and vibration each have their own switch in Settings.

How many levels and destinations are there?

291 real destinations on every continent, with 12 levels each: over 3,400 levels. Every city has its own tiles, a postcard and a stamp in Your Trip, and 23 famous landmarks wait along the way.

What do the boosters do?

Hint lights up your best move, Undo puts your last tile back on the board, Shuffle mixes up the tiles left, the Magic Wand clears a whole set and Extra Slot adds a fifth slot to the tray.

How do lives work?

A level only costs a life if you don’t win it. Clear the board and your lives stay just as they were.
