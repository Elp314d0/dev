How to Memorize English Vocabulary So It Sticks

Guide · Updated 2026-11-19

To memorize English vocabulary, connect each word to an image, hear and say it, then recall it yourself a few days later and again a few weeks later. Repetition spread over time beats any amount of cramming.

1. Learn the most common words first

A few hundred everyday words cover a large part of what you read and hear. Start with things you can see and touch: animals, food, the house, clothes.

2. Link the word to a picture, not a list

Lists of word = translation are hard to remember. A picture connects the English word straight to the thing.

3. Hear it and say it

Saying the word out loud gives your memory a second path, and you learn the pronunciation at the same time.

4. Recall instead of re-reading

Looking at a word again feels productive but does little. Trying to produce the word yourself, even when you fail, is what makes it stick.

5. Review on a schedule

Meet the word again after a few days, then after a few weeks. Each time it takes longer to forget.

How Word Search: Learning English does this for you

Your part is simple: play a little every day and say each word out loud.
